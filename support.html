ImmiGreen Support

We are here to help with setup, app behavior, and technical issues.

Where does the data come from?

ImmiGreen presents publicly available employment-based Visa Bulletin information. Confirm important decisions using official government sources or qualified legal counsel.

Does ImmiGreen provide legal advice?

No. ImmiGreen is an informational tracking tool and does not provide legal advice or guarantee an immigration outcome.

Where is my profile stored?

Your saved country, employment category, and priority date are stored locally on your device in the current version.

Why does the app show offline data?

If the latest feed cannot be downloaded, ImmiGreen may show previously downloaded bulletin information.

How do I enable notifications?

Allow notifications when prompted, or manage permissions under iPhone Settings → Notifications → ImmiGreen.

Report a bug

Email YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL with your iPhone model, iOS version, a description of the issue, and a screenshot when possible.

Response time

We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.
