Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Overview

ImmiGreen helps users follow employment-based U.S. Visa Bulletin information and track a priority date. The app is designed to minimize data collection.

Information stored on your device

ImmiGreen may store your country of chargeability, employment-based category, priority date, notification preferences, and cached Visa Bulletin information locally on your device.

Data collection

ImmiGreen does not require account registration, does not sell personal information, and does not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking in the current version.

Internet access

The app connects to the internet to download publicly available Visa Bulletin data using standard HTTPS networking provided by Apple platforms.

Notifications

If enabled, ImmiGreen may send local notifications related to newly available bulletin data or detected cutoff-date changes.

Government affiliation

ImmiGreen is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the U.S. Department of State, USCIS, or any other government agency.

Legal disclaimer

ImmiGreen provides informational tracking tools only and does not provide legal advice or guarantee immigration outcomes.

Your choices

You may delete saved information by removing your profile in the app or uninstalling the app.
